## Current interpretation
Search is a minor discovery source (1.5%), so SEO is hygiene rather than the main growth lever.

The strongest evidence is the pair of Shorts with the same title, “Jesus was only amazed twice.” One version has 44.4% stayed-to-watch and the other 30.7%. Because the title is held constant, the difference points strongly toward execution in the opening seconds — first frame, pacing, wording, visual, or delivery — rather than title alone.

A useful funnel distinction is emerging:
- Some Shorts win the stop: high stayed-to-watch.
- Some lose the stop but perform well once watched: strong AVD / looping.
- Improving the first second should be the next creative priority, especially for Shorts with low stayed-to-watch but strong AVD.

## Best current model
“Bible says women…” is the strongest all-around reference: 59.2% stayed-to-watch plus 0:15 AVD on a 0:10 Short. It combines a strong stop signal with replay behavior.
