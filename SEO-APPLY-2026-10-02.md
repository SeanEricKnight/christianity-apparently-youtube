# SEO changes to apply — 2026-10-02

Baseline is preserved in `BASELINE-2026-10-02.md`.

These changes are designed to improve topic clarity without turning the channel into keyword soup.

## 1) Channel description

Replace the current YouTube channel description with:

Short videos about Christianity, Jesus, the Bible, faith, doubt, grace, and the questions Christians sometimes answer too quickly.

I’m a Christian. I still have questions. This is a place to look honestly at Jesus without pretending the hard parts aren’t there.

Christianity, apparently.

https://christianityapparently.com

## 2) Channel keywords

If the Channel > Basic info > Keywords field is available, use:

Christianity, Jesus, Bible, Christian faith, faith and doubt, Christian questions, God, gospel, grace, theology, Bible questions, questions about Christianity, questions about Jesus, Christian Shorts

Do not add dozens of loosely related keywords.

## 3) Existing Shorts — description upgrades

Do not mass-rename titles yet. Preserve title performance as a separate experiment.

What does the Bible actually say about women? A short look at a difficult Christian question without pretending the difficult parts are not there.

### Does God love you?

Does God really love you? A short Christian question about God’s love, grace, faith, and what Jesus reveals about God.

## 4) Future upload rule

For each new Short:

- Keep the title human and compelling.
- Put the actual topic in the first sentence of the description.
- Use one or two important search terms naturally.
- Add Scripture references when the Short is built on a specific passage.
- Keep each description unique.
- Do not rely on tags for discovery.
- Do not stuff descriptions with repetitive keywords.
